# Recorded animal sounds — attribution and processing

These are real animal recordings, not synthesized calls. Original titles, creators, licenses, pinned download mirrors, license evidence and SHA256 hashes are provided in sources.json.

All excerpts were trimmed, gently faded, and normalized around -20 LUFS with -3 dBTP ceiling. Output is mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 128 kbps. The monkey and donkey calls repeat genuine short excerpts with a silent gap. No pitch shifting or generated animal voices.
