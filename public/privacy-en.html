Privacy policy

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026 · Version 1.0

Pre-publication review copy: the publisher’s identity and contact details must be completed, and this policy must be hosted at a public URL before release.

Little Play Garden is a collection of Persian educational games for children around ages 3–5 to enjoy with their families. This policy covers the Android app and the web version of this product.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, usage analytics or behavioral tracking. It does not collect a child’s name, date of birth, age, address, photograph, microphone audio, location, contacts, advertising identifier or game answers. The app does not send this information to publisher servers or advertising companies.

There are no child profiles, individual performance reports, leaderboards, chat or public sharing. The publisher cannot view a child’s answers through the app.

Preferences stored on the device

Only app preferences, such as volume, mute and the optional voice-guide setting, are saved in local device storage. The selected game and answers in a round exist only in temporary runtime memory; they are not saved as a child’s history. There is no cloud synchronization.

Use “Reset preferences” in the parents area to clear saved preferences. Clearing the app’s data in Android settings or uninstalling it also removes its local data. For the web version, clearing this website’s data in the browser removes local preferences.

Games, audio and device permissions

The Android app includes its games, pictures, font and animal recordings. After installation, playing this content does not require an internet connection. The web version needs a connection to retrieve website files initially.

Visual instructions are always available. In supported versions and devices, optional narration uses only bundled audio or an installed local speech voice. Narration availability depends on the app version and the device; it may be unavailable on some devices. The app does not use cloud speech services, listen to a child or record audio.

Playing does not require access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. The app does not request these permissions.

Internet access and links in the parents area

Asset sources and license information are available in the parents area. Opening an external link requires an adult’s action after the reading challenge. Links open in a browser and may need an internet connection. Destination websites and the browser may process information such as an IP address and technical request data under their own policies. This policy does not cover those services.

The app itself has no analytics, advertising network or cloud reporting service. The web version’s host may process ordinary web-request information when serving website files under that host’s own policies. The installed Android app does not fetch its game files from a website during play.

The role of the parents area

The reading challenge helps keep settings and links out of a young child’s direct reach. It is not adult identity verification, age verification or legally verifiable parental consent. The app does not ask for a child’s or parent’s identity to obtain consent. Access lasts at most five minutes and locks again when returning to the game or when the app goes into the background.

This collection supports educational play. It is not a medical diagnostic tool or a developmental assessment.
