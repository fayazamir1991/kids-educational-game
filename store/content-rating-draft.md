# Content-rating worksheet — draft

No IARC questionnaire has been submitted and no rating certificate has been issued for this build. The recommended learning audience, ages 3–5, is not an assigned store content rating. Complete the current live questionnaires using the final app and the publisher's own account.

## Content inventory for review

| Area | Current intended content | What to check in the final binary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gameplay | Eight simple picture-based games with cartoon animal friends, colors, shapes, fruit, counting, a puzzle and everyday actions | Confirm every accessible game and parent screen matches this inventory. |
| Violence or fear | No combat, weapons, injury or threat scenarios are designed into gameplay; animal calls and a brief red retry frame are present | Listen to every shipped call and inspect all animations for intensity and age suitability. |
| Sexual content or nudity | None designed into the games | Review all assets and parent documents before answering the exact live form questions. |
| Language | Persian gameplay prompts and parent information; no profanity is intended | Review bundled narration and all text. English is used in the optional store listing and privacy document. |
| Gambling or purchases | No gambling, real-money play, currency, loot boxes, ads or in-app purchases | Confirm no related SDK, billing code or promotion is bundled. Random celebration styles do not award items or currency. |
| User communication | No chat, accounts, user-generated uploads or sharing between players | Confirm no camera, microphone, location, contacts or public profile feature exists. |
| Internet and external destinations | Gameplay is bundled and offline; adult-gated source/support/privacy links may open in the system browser | Review all links and parent-gate behavior. Describe this accurately in app-access and content forms. |
| Child data | Only local preferences are planned; no child accounts, names, recordings or progress uploads | Complete final dependency, permission and network reviews before selecting no-collection answers. |

## Target audience

For Google Play's Target Audience and Content section, the planned choice is **Ages 5 & Under**, because the product is designed for children around ages 3–5. Do not select older groups simply to broaden distribution. A parent area does not make the game an all-ages app. Review [Google Play Families requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335?hl=en) before submission.

Supply the actual parent-gate instructions in the reviewer app-access notes. Do not give reviewers a nonexistent login or claim special access is needed for the eight games when no accounts exist.
